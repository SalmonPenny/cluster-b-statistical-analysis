## Estimands and mathematical interpretation

**Matched effects.** The arithmetic uses the printed scores with the sign chosen by the outcome direction. Percentage-point conversion multiplies proportion differences by 100, while percent differences are already in points. Relative performance uses a positive baseline. Error reduction uses the baseline's remaining error probability. Undesirable-event reduction uses the baseline event rate. Blank values preserve undefined or inapplicable quantities. In RMM's GTE contrast the judged QA gain is 6.8 pp, relative accuracy improves by 10.69%, and the judged error complement falls by 18.68%. That complement covers incorrect answers and supplies no direct hallucination rate. The SIRIN relative event arithmetic describes the served-answer populations, whose composition changes under gating. All 14 assumption-conditional products reconcile. Under a simple unweighted binary count over the nominal served count, 12 of the 14 percentages cannot round to the displayed conditional rate. The source does not establish the cause, so the empirical all-query field remains blank.

**Aggregation and failure modes.** The stratum includes the study, table, protocol, explicit reference, outcome and scale. Endpoints and pairs are deduplicated within the relevant grouping. Singleton strata eliminate the leverage of reweighting. The pipeline's locality-free screening provides an additional check, but any claim of transportability would still need independent protocol and source review. Equal point estimates across weighting schemes supply no evidence that architectures have equal performance, and a matrix gap supplies no effect estimate. Correlated submetrics, shared baseline cells and overlapping question sets remain dependence sources even when rows have distinct observation IDs.

**Ranks and Pareto.** Ranks are descriptive competition ranks with shared rank and skipped positions for ties. Complete compatible vectors are compared after orienting all metrics to improvement. Ordinary Pareto dominance requires no losses and at least one gain. SIRIN coverage comes from complementing the published abstention percentages, separately from the strict Stage 2A effects. It changes the operational choice and does not establish a common denominator for all-query unfaithfulness. Different metrics from the same evaluation remain correlated even when complete.

**Tolerance and objective sensitivity.** At tolerance \(\tau\), a pair dominates if at least one oriented improvement exceeds \(\tau\) and no oriented loss exceeds \(\tau\). This relation can be non-transitive. Tolerance edges are counted separately, and standard Pareto fronts use zero tolerance. Mutually exclusive response categories in HaluMem and IMLogic are coupled, so weighting them cannot produce multiple independent confirmations. Changing objective weights uses normalized within-panel utility, analytical two-objective intervals and seed-27 simplex draws. Weight shares describe hypothetical preferences. Neither grid stability nor a simplex share measures sampling uncertainty. A monotone rescaling preserves zero-tolerance order, but relative changes across different baseline denominators need not preserve effect-size order.

**Pooled ranking.** A Bradley–Terry ability difference requires a common win outcome and an aligned comparison design. The projected architecture graph contains local edges across configurations, tasks and judges. A reference constraint handles location invariance, and a penalty can stabilize a fit, but neither creates exchangeable comparisons or independent replications. Correlated within-study scores cannot be converted into independent wins. No probabilistic ranking was fitted.
